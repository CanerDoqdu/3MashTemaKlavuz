Maliyet Detay Sayfası

Bu bölümde​

  • Yapılandırmada kayıtlı 105 alanın türü, başlangıç değeri ve grubu bulunur.
  • Alan değerleri tema yapılandırmasından alınmıştır; mağazandaki güncel değerler farklı olabilir.

Ne işe yarar?​

🌐 İngilizce Maliyet Açıklaması Metni (explanationBodyEn)RICH_TEXTA common misconception in the industry is that the cost of a remake is measured only by the re-lab fee . 🌐 İngilizce Klinik Tekrar Verisi İçeriği (studyOneContentEn)RICH_TEXTPEER-REVIEWED CLINICAL DATA What is the remake rate really? The National PBRN measured it across 3,750 crowns. McCracken M.S. et al. (National Dental PBRN Collaborative Group) · Journal of Prosthodontics , 2019;28(2):122–130 205 dentists evaluated 3,750 single-unit crowns in real clinical practice. Average remake rate is 3.8% — but varies from 0% to 42% across practitioners. While 58% of clinicians never reject a crown, all rejections came from the remaining 42% — meaning the problem is single and solvable . Most common rejection causes: proximal misfit, marginal errors, and aesthetic failure — all direct dimensional accuracy problems. 🌐 İngilizce Ölçüsel Doğruluk Araştırması İçeriği (studyTwoContentEn)RICH_TEXTPEER-REVIEWED · DIMENSIONAL ACCURACY So why does deviation occur? Accuracy varies end-to-end depending on the system used. Etemad-Shahidi Y. et al. · J Clin Med 2020 (systematic review) · Németh A. et al. · J Dentistry 2023 (network meta-analysis) In the systematic review, full-arch model accuracy with the best system goes down to 3.3 µm , while on some devices it goes up to 130–190 µm — meaning the device and parameters determine the correct result. The network meta-analysis ranks SLA, DLP, and PolyJet as the most accurate technologies (SLA ~ 86.7 µm , DLP ~ 97.9 µm average trueness). Deviation is not chance; it is a manageable variable. 3mash calibrates these variables together to deliver ±20 µm on every print , lowering remake rate and cost.
